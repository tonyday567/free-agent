# design deck · surfacing primitives

**free** ⟜ Loop (feedback), Poly (state), Ends (channels) — read, match, transform before `run`.

**polymorphic** ⟜ tensor + arrow are parameters. Same combinator for pure/Kleisli/lazy/iterative.

**open·close** ⟜ strength↔trace, open↔close, encode↔run. Every open has a close. Adjunctions, not the lead.

**crown** ⟜ retry (STM quiescence), fork (id oracle), par (disjoint wires), ambient (threaded state). Taxonomy IS library.

**axioma** ⟜ laws compile, green lights gate. Admission: primary source with compiling axioms + exact check?

**machina** ⟜ diagnose, don't judge. timer→that, Core→how, allocation→why. Gap classification. Thin core, fat machina.

**substrate** ⟜ depends on everything. substrate-green-lights proves compile+link. ~/haskell/ core+axioma, ~/machina/ measurement.

**house** ⟜ free over fixed, polymorphic over privileged, concrete-first, short names, defaults-first.
